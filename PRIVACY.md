# Privacy

NeuroMind Atlas is designed to minimize collection.

- Theme and language preferences are stored locally in the visitor's browser.
- The optional mood tracker stores only a calendar date and a numeric rating from 1 to 5 in the browser. It does not accept or retain free-text notes, names, diagnoses, or treatment information.
- The mood-history control can remove that local record at any time.
- Fonts and interface icons are served with the site; normal page loading does not contact a font or icon CDN.
- Academic, institutional, and search links contact their destination only after the visitor chooses them. Those destinations apply their own privacy policies.

Clearing site data in the browser removes the stored preferences and mood history.

This policy describes the repository release dated 2026-09-23. The hosting provider may retain limited network and operational logs under its own terms.
