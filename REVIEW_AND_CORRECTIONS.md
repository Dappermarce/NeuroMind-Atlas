# Review and corrections

## Publication status

NeuroMind Atlas is an independently authored educational software publication. It is not a diagnostic tool, clinical guideline, systematic review, or peer-reviewed journal article. It may be cited as software using `CITATION.cff`, but a citation does not make the atlas a substitute for primary evidence or professional care.

## Evidence and review

Major claims should be traceable to an inline source or the bibliography. Historical sources are distinguished from current clinical evidence, and diagnostic classifications are described as professional frameworks rather than self-assessment tools. Any future professional or academic review must state the reviewer's name, relevant credentials, date, scope, conflicts of interest, and requested changes before the site describes itself as externally reviewed.

## Corrections

Corrections should identify the affected claim, the supporting primary or high-quality synthesis source, and the reason for the change. Material changes should be recorded in a versioned GitHub release. Superseded statements should be corrected or removed rather than silently preserved as current guidance.

For durable academic citation, archive a numbered release in a preservation service such as Zenodo and cite the resulting DOI together with the version and access date.
